The Opportunity

A Rare 98-Acre Estate in Central Minnesota

My dad, Jack McCoy, ran Circle R Ranch — a kids horse camp — on these 98 acres in Long Prairie for over 50 years (1969–2025). Generations of central Minnesota kids learned to ride here every summer. Dad passed last year. My three siblings and I have wound down the camp and are selling the real estate direct from our family.

Over five decades Dad put up the buildings that are still on the property today: horse barns and stables, a covered riding arena (uninsulated — three-season cover), fenced pastures, an in-ground heated pool, a dining hall with full commercial kitchen, multiple cabins and bunkhouses sleeping 85, staff housing, two silos, his main 2-story farmhouse, ample parking, and direct State Highway 27 access. The buildings, infrastructure, ponds, and tillable ground are intact and ready for whatever the next owner builds.

Whether that's a private equestrian estate, multi-generational family compound, faith-based camp, retreat or wellness center, hunting and recreation property, wedding venue, or something nobody's thought of yet — the foundation is already poured. Three Twin Cities-region airports within reach. Gigabit fiber to the property. No broker. No commission. Direct from our family to yours.

FAQ

Common Questions

What's the asking price and is it negotiable?

$2,750,000 for the full 98-acre estate including all buildings, improvements, and parcels. Serious offers will be considered — we are sole sellers, not bound by commission obligations. Seller financing and partial-property options may be available to qualified buyers.

Why is it being sold?

The longtime owner passed in 2025 and his children are settling the estate. The continuous operating business that was on the property has been wound down. The property is being sold for its land, location, and facilities — not as an operating business.

Is the existing business included? Can I buy the brand?

No. This is a real-estate sale only. The previous brand and operating business are not part of the offering and are not licensed to the buyer. The buyer is free to operate a new business under any name they choose.

What's the building condition?

Buildings were maintained throughout continuous operation. Specific condition reports, age, square footage, and inspection records are available to qualified buyers. We strongly encourage a full inspection during your due-diligence period.

What about utilities, well, septic?

Well-served by Todd-Wadena Electric Cooperative for power. CTC Telecommunications offers gigabit fiber in the Long Prairie corridor (final hookup to the property to be verified with CTC). Septic and well are private — certificates and recent water test available upon request.

What's the zoning?

Todd County agricultural district. Many uses are by-right; some uses (commercial event venue, retreat center, certain commercial operations) may require a Conditional Use Permit. Buyers planning a specific use should confirm with Todd County Planning & Zoning before purchase.

How are property taxes?

Todd County property tax rates run 0.88-0.99% — well below the Minnesota average. Combined annual property tax across all parcels in scope is modest. Specific tax statements are available on request.

Can I subdivide?

Todd County subdivision ordinance generally allows lot splits with appropriate SSTS (septic) approval. State trunk highway frontage typically supports 2-4 splits depending on configuration. Buyers should consult with Todd County P&Z to confirm specific subdivision plans.

Are showings available?

Yes — I'll work around your schedule. Weekday, weekend, evening, all open. Out-of-state buyers welcome to schedule day-long tours; we can coordinate with local hotels. Drone tour and additional photos available before any in-person showing.

Is the seller open to 1031 exchanges?

Yes. The property qualifies as like-kind real estate. We are familiar with 1031 timelines and will work with your QI to close within the required windows. Bring your QI's contact info to the first conversation.
